this is example of route group.
how to create?

create folder > use brackets > add folder and page > Route group created
ex. (auth)  > login -page.tsx
            > register -page.tsx

the way to access them is simple.
it will not change routes.
the way to access route will be 
ex.
- localhost/login
- localhost/register

here: auth will group it, it will not take any part in changeing the route
